Not Signal

Signal is a US nonprofit, subject to US law. Its centralized infrastructure is a single point of failure, legal or technical. Zmey places the same cryptographic guarantees in Swiss jurisdiction.

Not Discord

Not a community platform with a privacy label. Zmey is messaging, direct and group conversations app, with mandatory end-to-end encryption, key verification, and no metadata harvesting. Fewer features, zero compromise.

Not compliant

The EU's Chat Control regulation demands client-side scanning. Zmey's architecture makes that technically impossible. No backdoor. No scanning. No exceptions.

How it works

01

Matrix protocol

Open, federated, auditable. Your messages travel through infrastructure you can verify. No proprietary black boxes. No trust required, only math.

02

Swiss jurisdiction

Hosted in Switzerland. Not subject to the CLOUD Act, not subject to Chat Control, not subject to FISA. Swiss Federal Data Protection Act applies one of the strongest privacy frameworks in the world.

03

One operator

Run by a single person. No board, no investors, no stakeholders with competing interests. Minimal attack surface. The infrastructure is the policy, there is nothing to read, nothing to hand over, nothing to compromise.
