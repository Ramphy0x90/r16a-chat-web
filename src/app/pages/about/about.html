Why this exists

Privacy shouldn't depend
on legal exemptions.

Chat Control

On July 9, 2026, the EU extended its voluntary CSAM-scanning derogation, the legal basis that lets platforms scan your private messages without a court order. The more severe mandatory version, Chat Control 2.0, is still under negotiation. Today's voluntary exemption is tomorrow's legal requirement. We build for the architecture, not the current political weather.

Why not Signal?

Signal's cryptography is excellent. But the Signal Technology Foundation is a US entity, subject to US legal process. Jurisdiction matters. An European, Swiss-hosted, community-governed alternative isn't redundant. It's a genuinely different trust proposition.

What makes it different

Architecture, not promises.

01

Self-hosted

Running on personally-owned hardware in Switzerland. No AWS, no GCP, no cloud provider with their own compliance obligations and data access frameworks.

02

Matrix protocol

Built on an open standard with independently audited end-to-end encryption (Olm/Megolm via vodozemac). Not custom homegrown crypto(I was tempted), battle-tested, peer-reviewed cryptography.

03

Zero-knowledge operator

The operator has no technical means to read message content. This is a structural fact of the encryption design, not a policy promise that can be revised.

04

One operator

Run by a single person. No board, no investors, no stakeholders with competing interests. Minimal attack surface. The infrastructure is the policy.
