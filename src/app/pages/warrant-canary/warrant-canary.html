Warrant Canary

Canary statement.

A warrant canary is a periodic statement that's true only as long as it keeps being published on schedule. Some legal processes such as US National Security Letters, come bundled with a gag order: the recipient cannot disclose that the request was made. The only signal available is absence, the canary simply stops updating.

This mechanism has not been definitively tested in court. It is a meaningful signal, not a legal guarantee.
